Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our digital health platform.

How do I access my medical records?

Sign in to your patient portal and open Medical Records to see the diagnoses, notes and prescriptions your doctors have added.

Is my health data secure and private?

Your medical record notes are stored encrypted, and Health Vault documents are visible only to you and the doctors you choose.

How do I book an appointment?

Sign in, choose Book Appointment, pick a doctor, a date and a time, and add a short reason. Your doctor then confirms the appointment.
